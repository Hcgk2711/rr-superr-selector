TYPE 1 OPTION • AUDITED FIRST PRIORITY

APPROACH 1: BASELINE VS. PRESSURE DIFFERENTIAL (RCD)

Why Approach 1 Leads the Strategy

Approach 1 operationalises the core scouting question: does this player raise their game when the situation demands it? A player who strikes at 150 normally but drops to 125 under pressure is exposed; a player who rises from 145 to 195 is a clutch asset. To protect Rajasthan Royals against misleading small samples, this model has been strictly audited to remove tailenders, micro-sample outliers, and associate-league skews.

Batter Pressure Triggers (10,761 Balls):
  • P1: Chasing with Required Run Rate ≥ 9.00 rpo.
  • P2: Death overs (16–20) of a live chase with ≤ 36 runs needed.
  • P3: Wickets in hand ≤ 4 with ≤ 30 balls remaining.
  • P4: Death overs of an IPL/T20I knockout match.
Bowler Pressure Triggers (19,612 Balls):
  • Q1: Defending when opposition's RRR ≥ 9.00 rpo.
  • Q2: Death overs (16–20) of a defending innings.
  • Q3: Defending with ≤ 12 balls left and ≤ 12 runs needed.
  • Q4: Death overs of a playoff / knockout clash.
Audited Clutch Index Formulas (Z-Score Standardised):
Batters: Clutchbat = 0.40 × z(pSR) + 0.25 × z(pAvg) + 0.15 × z(Death SR) + 0.10 × z(Boundary%) + 0.10 × z(Volume)
Bowlers: Clutchbowl = 0.30 × z(Pressure Wkts) + 0.25 × z(Death Dot%) + 0.20 × z(Pressure Econ) + 0.15 × z(Econ Improve) + 0.10 × z(Volume)

TYPE 2 OPTION • CONTINUOUS DLS-LEVERAGE MODEL

APPROACH 2: COMPOSITE PRESSURE INDEX (CPI)

Continuous In-Game Leverage

Instead of binary trigger flags, Approach 2 assigns a continuous pressure score from 0.00 to 1.00 to every single ball based on match deficit, resources remaining, and phase. Performance is evaluated exclusively on the top-quartile high-pressure deliveries (CPI ≥ 0.066) across all 200,999 balls in the database.

TYPE 3 OPTION • SITUATION-SPECIFIC ROLE FIT

APPROACH 3: SITUATION-SPECIFIC ROLE SCENARIOS (TREI)

Concrete Tactical Roles

Approach 3 recognizes that a death-overs finisher's job (chase closing in overs 16–20) is fundamentally different from a top-order anchor rebuilding after early wickets. It isolates 5 distinct tactical scenarios that decide IPL championships, scoring each player on their specific assigned role.

S1: DEATH BATTING

Overs 16–20, any innings. Late acceleration.

Weight: 35% Bat
S2: CHASE FINISH

2nd innings, overs 16–20, ≤ 30 runs needed.

Weight: 45% Bat
S3: PP REBUILD

Powerplay with ≥ 1 wkt down by over 4.

Weight: 20% Bat
S4: DEATH BOWLING

Overs 16–20 of 2nd innings defence.

Weight: 65% Bowl
S5: LOW-TOTAL DEF

Defending target ≤ 150 runs.

Weight: 35% Bowl
