MATHEMATICAL SPECIFICATION

DATA ARCHITECTURE & FORMULAS

Interactive mathematical specification: Hover over either equation to see kinetic displacement animation.

EQUATION 01 BATTER CLUTCH FORMULA
Clutchbat = 0.40 × z(SRpressure) + 0.25 × z(Avgpressure) + 0.15 × z(Death SR) + 0.10 × z(Boundary%) + 0.10 × z(Volume)

Pressure strike rate and average carry the heaviest weight because a true clutch finisher must score rapidly while staying not out to close chases. Death SR ensures direct overs 16–20 execution.

EQUATION 02 BOWLER CLUTCH FORMULA
Clutchbowl = 0.30 × z(Pressure Wkts) + 0.25 × z(Death Dot%) + 0.20 × z(Pressure Econ) + 0.15 × z(Econ Improve) + 0.10 × z(Volume)

Pressure wicket volume and death dot-ball percentage lead, because defensive containment alone without taking wickets does not close out IPL matches. Economy improvement rewards bowlers who tighten when it matters.
